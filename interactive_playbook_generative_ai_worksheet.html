Task 2

Do Now! Vocabulary Challenge: Live Critique

Allocated: 04:00 04:00

Scenario: AI Art Prompt Output Review

The teacher entered the prompt: "A dog playing basketball on the moon in cartoon style". Analyze the prompt output and construct your critique using the target terms: Claim, Assertion, and Viewpoint.

Target Term 1

Claim

An unproven statement or representation made by the generator.

Target Term 2

Assertion

A confident statement of fact or strong belief regarding the output.

Target Term 3

Viewpoint

A specific stance or perspective from which something is evaluated.

💡 Hint: Use 'Claim' for what the AI claims to depict, 'Assertion' for your definitive observation (e.g., gravity or fur detail), and 'Viewpoint' for a artist's or scientist's perspective.
