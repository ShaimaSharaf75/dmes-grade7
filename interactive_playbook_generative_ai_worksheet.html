Lesson Concept 01

What is Generative Artificial Intelligence?

Unlike traditional software or search engines that simply retrieve saved files, Generative AI uses complex neural models trained on billions of existing images and text. It learns underlying patterns to construct brand new artwork, code, or written content from scratch.

Search Engine (e.g. Google)

Finds and links to artwork that human artists have already created and saved on the web.

Generative AI (e.g. Artguru)

Synthesizes brand new pixels pixel-by-pixel based on patterns it learned during training.

Lesson Concept 02

The Artguru AI Prompting Formula

Generative AI tools rely on clear, descriptive instructions. Vague inputs like "a dog" produce unpredictable results. To get precise outputs, use the 3-part formula:

[ Clear Subject ] + [ Art Style / Medium ] + [ Lighting & Color Details ]
1. Subject "An astronaut playing electric guitar on Mars"
2. Art Style "80s synthwave neon, cyberpunk digital art"
3. Lighting & Details "Dramatic rim lighting, glowing purple dust"
Lesson Concept 03

How AI Models Synthesize Images

Image models (like Diffusion systems) start with a screen full of completely random visual noise (like television static). They gradually remove the noise step-by-step, shaping the pixels to match the patterns associated with the words in your text prompt.

1 Random Pixel Static
2 Noise Reduction Pass
3 Detailed High-Res Canvas
Lesson Concept 04

Ethics, Copyright & Fairness in AI Art

Because AI models learn from human artwork, serious ethical and legal questions arise regarding artist copyright, original creativity, and ownership of computer-generated images.

Copyright & Artist Rights

Many artists argue that training AI on their artwork without consent or compensation is unfair.

Responsible AI Usage

Always give credit when presenting AI-generated work and avoid impersonating living artists directly.
